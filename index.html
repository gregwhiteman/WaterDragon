Master numbers 11, 22, and 33 are kept, and also read as 11/2, 22/4, 33/6. Glynis adds the digits of each date part. Gary adds every digit, and he also adds month + day + year as whole numbers (2 + 8 + 2008 = 2018 → 11). He keeps 11, 22, or 33 from either pass. He keeps the 11th and 22nd as master days instead of splitting them.
